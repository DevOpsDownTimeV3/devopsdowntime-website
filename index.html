DevOps Downtime V3

A capture-the-flag afternoon for people who keep things running.

Where the flags are

Three areas, one environment. Every flag sits behind a mistake that turns up in real infrastructure.

  • Servers and their config
  • Pipelines and Kubernetes
  • Cloud infrastructure

Event Schedule

Where: Leapfrog Connect, Kathmandu.

  • 12:00 Check-in
  • 1:00 Welcome & rules (Wi-Fi, scoreboard login, LLM policy)
  • 1:15 Game on!
  • 3:15 Final scores
  • 3:30 Winners
  • 3:45 Networking, close by 4 PM

Rules

  • You play solo. One person, one laptop.
  • Stay in your environment.
  • Hints cost points: 10% for a nudge, 25% for a bigger hint.
  • Docs, man pages, search engines and LLMs are all fair game.
  • Ties go to whoever reached the score first.

How to get in

Registration comes with an entry challenge. Find the entry flag, then paste it into the registration form.

Eligibility

Your flag will be screened to test if you can take on the challenge. Limited seats for eligible participants only.
